It is a country brimming with romance, thrill and adventure – nothing says “classic safari” quite like a Kenyan safari.

Why Visit Kenya?

Kenya is one of Africa’s most widely-recognised safari destinations
, boasting famous wilderness areas like Amboseli National Park,
Tsavo, and, of course, the legendary Masai Mara.
Here, sweeping savannahs and dramatic wildlife
spectacles await for anyone willing to leave the city behind.

THE HOME OF THE SAFARI

Explore Kenya and be enchanted by the strength and majesty of lions crossing your way in the national parks, be mesmerised by the colourful underwater world of the Indian Ocean, be touched by the natural beauty of Mount Kenya. Be ready – to experience a trip of a lifetime! We live, travel and work here and are lucky enough to call Kenya home. Join us on one of our tours, exploring the home of the safari.

DISCOVER KENYA’S STUNNING WILDERNESS AREAS

The annual Great Migration in the Maasai Mara, the flocks of pink flamingos at Lake Nakuru and the calm warm waters at the Kenyan coast have attracted generations of travellers. On a Kenya safari, you can experience diverse landscapes from lush forests and wide grasslands to a 500-kilometre-long coastline without crossing borders or having to get on a plane.
